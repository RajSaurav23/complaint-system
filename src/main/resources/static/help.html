How to Use the Portal

Step 1: Register/Login into the portal.
Step 2: Click on "Register Complaint".
Step 3: Fill complaint details and upload image (if required).
Step 4: Submit complaint.
Step 5: Track complaint status from dashboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How can I track my complaint?
A: Login and go to dashboard.

Q: Can I upload images?
A: Yes, image upload is supported.

Q: How long does resolution take?
A: It depends on complaint type but is processed as soon as possible.
